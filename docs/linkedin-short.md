## ON THE EMAIL GATE

Gating the prompt is fine and it will convert — the people who want a prompt for
a data-integrity build are close to the people who buy analysis. Two things worth
getting right:

1. **Do not gate the game.** Gate the prompt only. If someone has to give an
   email to check whether your numbers are real, the post's whole argument dies.
   The claim is "go and test me" — that has to be one tap, no wall.

2. **Say what they get, plainly.** "The prompt" is vague and undersells it. The
   honest, more valuable offer is the method: the prompt, the facts file, and the
   integrity gate that enforces it. That is the thing nobody else is handing out.
